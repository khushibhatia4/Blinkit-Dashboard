# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard: Outlet & Product Performance Insights

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Blinkit’s sales performance across outlets, product categories, outlet sizes, and locations to support business and operational decision-making.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

The **Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of sales, product performance, outlet efficiency, and customer ratings.

It enables stakeholders to understand **revenue drivers, outlet performance, and product demand patterns** using interactive visualizations.

---

# 🛠 Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Building interactive dashboards and KPI cards  
- 📂 **Power Query** – Data cleaning, transformation, and shaping  
- 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used for KPIs like total sales, average sales, ratings, and item counts  
- 🧩 **Data Modeling** – Structured relationships between outlet, product, and sales data  
- 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for development and `.png` for dashboard preview  

---

# 📂 Data Source

**Source:** Blinkit Retail Sales Dataset  

The dataset includes information on:

- Outlet type
- Item categories
- Fat content
- Outlet size
- Outlet location type
- Sales amount
- Customer ratings
- Item visibility

This data enables **end-to-end retail sales analysis**.

---

# 🚩 Business Problem

Quick-commerce platforms like **Blinkit** operate across multiple outlet types and locations.  

Without analytics, it becomes difficult to answer questions such as:

- Which outlet types generate the highest sales?
- Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
- How does outlet size and location affect performance?
- What product attributes influence customer ratings and sales?

---

# 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The primary goal of this dashboard is to:

- Provide a **centralized sales performance view**
- Identify **top-performing outlets and products**
- Support **inventory planning and outlet expansion**
- Assist **marketing and operational decision-making**

# 📊 Dashboard Walkthrough

Key metrics displayed at the top of the dashboard:

---

## 📈 Outlet Establishment Trend (Area Chart)

- Shows **sales trends over time**
- Highlights **peak performance around 2018**
- Indicates **stable growth in later years**

---

## 🍽 Sales by Item Type (Bar Chart)

- **Fruits & Vegetables** and **Snack Foods** are top revenue contributors
- Helps identify **high-demand product categories**

---

## 🥗 Fat Content Analysis (Donut + Bar Charts)

- Compares **Low Fat vs Regular product sales**
- Tier-wise analysis shows how fat content performs across different outlet locations

---

## 🏪 Outlet Size Analysis (Donut Chart)

- **Medium-sized outlets contribute the highest sales**
- Small and large outlets contribute comparatively less

---

## 📍 Outlet Location Analysis (Bar Chart)

- **Tier 3 locations generate the highest sales**
- Followed by **Tier 2 and Tier 1 locations**

This highlights **strong demand in semi-urban markets**.

---

## 🏬 Outlet Type Performance (Table View)

Compares:

- Grocery Stores
- Supermarket Types

Metrics analyzed:

- Total Sales
- Number of Items
- Average Rating
- Average Sales
- Item Visibility

**Supermarket Type 1 emerges as the top-performing outlet type.**

# 📈 Business Impact & Insights

### Revenue Optimization
Identify **high-performing outlet types and locations** to increase revenue.

### Inventory Planning
Stock more **high-demand product categories**.

### Expansion Strategy
Tier 3 locations demonstrate **strong sales potential for future expansion**.

### Customer Experience
An average rating of **3.9** indicates **consistent service quality**.

---

# 🔍 Overall Insights

- Blinkit achieves strong sales performance with **$1.20M total revenue**
- **Medium-sized outlets and Tier 3 locations** drive maximum sales
- **Fruits, snacks, and household items** dominate revenue
- **Supermarkets outperform grocery stores** in sales and item volume
